Put HarshOnTime on your home screen

On iPhone, HarshOnTime only works from the home-screen icon, not from a Safari tab — so setting up your identity has to happen there, not in this tab.

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Open HarshOnTime from the new icon and carry on there.
