HarshOnTime phone check

A five-minute test so we know notifications reach every phone before we build the real thing.

1 Put it on your home screen Done

On iPhone, notifications only work from the home-screen icon, not from a Safari tab.

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose “Add to Home Screen”.
  4. Open HarshOnTime from the new icon and carry on there.

In Chrome, open the ⋮ menu and choose “Add to Home screen” or “Install app”, then open HarshOnTime from the new icon.

Notifications also work from a normal Chrome tab on Android, so you can skip this for the test.
